## Team Page Design (for the Website)

### Layout
- **Hero:** "The people behind the numbers"
- **Core Team:** Two large cards with photos, bios, and responsibilities
- **Advisory Board:** Grid of 4 advisor cards with photos, expertise tags, and review focus
- **Contributors:** Collapsible list or smaller grid
- **Join Us:** Call to action for data contributors, translators, and researchers

### Content Strategy
- **Transparency:** Show real photos, real names, real affiliations. No stock photos.
- **Credibility:** Academic credentials, institutional affiliations, and publication records are front and center.
- **Diversity:** The advisory board should span 4+ continents and include voices from the Global South.
- **Accessibility:** Every photo has alt text. Bios are concise (100-150 words) and translatable.

### Translations
All bios and role descriptions must be translated into all 5 languages (EN, TH, ZH, KO, JA). This is a significant effort but essential for the Thai and Chinese audiences, who are the largest user groups.

---

## Photo Requirements

- **Core team:** Professional headshot, 600x800, neutral background, friendly expression
- **Advisory board:** Professional headshot, 400x500, consistent crop style
- **Contributors:** Optional photo, or a default avatar if they prefer not to share
- **Format:** WebP with JPG fallback
- **Size:** Core team < 100KB, advisors < 50KB each

### Where to Show the Team

1. **About Page ("About the Team"):**

2. **Home Page (Footer or "About" section):**
